Native. No browser inside.

A C++ app with one Qt Quick interface on Linux, macOS and Windows. It sits in your tray, starts fast, and works from your terminal too.

speecher listen prints what you say
Files
speecher transcribe turns audio and video into text
Calls
speecher record start writes a transcript, with you as "me" and the call as "them"

Also on Android.

A dictation button beside your keyboard, signed in to the same Claude or ChatGPT account. Tap, talk, insert. Scan a QR code to bring over your vocabulary and profiles; accounts and keys never leave your computer. Android 12 or newer.
