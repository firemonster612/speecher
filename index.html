Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Speecher is open source under the MIT license. Speech and cleanup run on a subscription you already have, a local model, or your own server.

Which services does it use?

Claude or ChatGPT, so either sign-in is enough. Setup asks which one turns your voice into words and which one cleans them up. You can add a backup for when one is rate limited, and if every cleanup fails, your raw words still land. The Claude and ChatGPT voice services are used unofficially, so they could change.

Where does the text go?

Where your cursor was, in any app. If you switch windows mid-sentence it stays on the clipboard. It never types into password fields, and it reads the field back to check the text arrived. Linux and Windows terminals get Ctrl Shift V, and your words stay copied.

Can it change what I've already written?

Yes. Select it, press the shortcut and say the change, like "change Friday to Monday". You see the edit first, and Enter puts it in place. It needs cleanup on and an app that shares its selection.

What gets sent where?

Your audio goes to the speech service you picked. The transcript, the app's name, the window title and the text around your cursor go to the cleanup service. If you turn on screenshot context, which is off by default, a picture of the screen goes too. By default, History keeps your text 90 days.

Does the phone need its own sign-in?

Yes. The Android keyboard signs in to Claude or ChatGPT itself. Your profiles and words can come over from your computer by QR code, on the same Wi-Fi.

Why does Linux ask for my password?

Pasting on Wayland needs a small helper, and installing it asks for your password once. Without it, your text goes to the clipboard and you paste it yourself.

Before you install
  • Linux. An x86-64 AppImage. Needs glibc 2.43 or newer, like Fedora 44, Ubuntu 26.04 or Debian 14. Pasting on Wayland needs a one-time helper, which asks for your password.
  • macOS. macOS 15 or newer. The app isn't signed yet, so Gatekeeper asks once.
  • Windows. Windows 11. Also unsigned, so SmartScreen asks once.
  • The pill. This page shows the pill from the nightly build. Version 0.3.0 still draws the earlier ribbon.
  • Android. A dictation keyboard, version 0.2.0, installed from GitHub rather than the Play Store.
