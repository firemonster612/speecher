  1. Talk the way you talk. The ums and restarts stay out of the text.

  4. Correct a word right after it lands and Speecher keeps the fix, locally.

Or keep it all on your machine.

Speecher downloads a speech model and runs it on your computer. No account, and no network once the model is on disk. It reads your GPU and memory and suggests a model that fits.

  • Speech runs inside Speecher. On the GPU through Metal on macOS and Vulkan on Linux and Windows.
  • Cleanup uses the runner you have. Ollama, LM Studio or llama-server, detected on their usual ports.
  • Or point it at your own server. Any OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint, and OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible cleanup.
