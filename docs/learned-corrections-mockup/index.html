How a correction is learned and surfaced

  1. Insert sends “ping the team on speaker today” into the field. Just before the commit, the keyboard reads up to 24 characters on each side of the cursor.
  2. The panel switches back to the person's keyboard as it does now. The dictation button's service reads the field from 2 s after Insert, every 2.5 s, for 30 s. Two matching readings with an edit (“speaker” → “Speecher”) make one observation. When nothing follows the dictation, the end of the field pins it on that side.
  3. Nothing appears in the moment, the same as on desktop: no toast, no panel message. A case-only fix such as “github” → “GitHub” is learned on the first observation. Any other fix needs to happen twice.
  4. Once learned, the correction replaces the heard word in later dictations, its spelling goes to the speech service and refinement, and it shows on the page above and on Insights.
