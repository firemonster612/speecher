Desktop accessibility

Step 4 of 9

Speecher pastes your dictation into the app you are using, and reads the text around your cursor so cleanup understands the context. On Linux both work through the desktop accessibility service (AT-SPI); it also lets Speecher learn your corrections.

  • Paste into the app you are using Allowed Blocked
  • Read the text around your cursor for context Allowed Blocked

Accessibility is off, so Speecher can copy your dictation but not paste it or see context. Turn it on to continue.

Desktop accessibility is enabled permanently.

This only affects what this app can do. You can turn it off any time in Settings.
