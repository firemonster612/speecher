The ratings

Every option is rated by the same two rules, from published benchmarks and our own timings. Each rounds to the nearest half.

  • Accuracy (speech) is 10 minus a point for each percentage point of words wrong above 2%, about the best any service scores. Word error rates are AA-WER v2 from Artificial Analysis.
  • Quality (refinement) is the share of checks passed on 15 messy transcripts, 3 runs each, through the app's real prompt and default settings: corrections applied, fillers and false starts gone, a dictated question or request left unanswered, numbers, paths and lists formatted.
  • Custom Endpoint gets no ratings at all: the server and model are the user's own.
  • Speed is 10 minus two points for each second you wait after you stop, for about 10 s of speech, and one more point off when no text shows while you speak.

What the numbers say

  • Speech. ChatGPT Codex makes about half as many mistakes as Claude Voice (3.3% of words against 6.6%), so it rates 8.5 to 5.5. Claude Voice is faster, 10 to 7: its text is final 0.07 s after you stop, where Codex waits for its last phrase (0.81 s) and then checks the whole recording again (0.77 s).
  • Refinement. OpenAI and Anthropic both passed all 123 checks, so quality ties at 10. OpenAI is faster: 1.5 s against 2.8 s at the median, and 2.6 s against 4.9 s at the slow end. Anthropic's fast mode needs usage credits, which a subscription doesn't have; the app falls back to standard speed, so that is what was timed.
  • Local cleanup models miss about half the checks: they leave "no wait" corrections in, keep spoken numbers as words and skip lists.

Open questions

  • Refinement quality ties. Both cloud providers aced the checks. A harder set would be needed to separate them; until then the bars say they clean up equally well.
  • Missing benchmark numbers. Artificial Analysis lists three of the seven local models. Parakeet uses Parakeet TDT 0.6B V2's figure. Moonshine and Qwen3-ASR are estimated from FLEURS English, scaled by how the three listed models compare on both benchmarks. Qwen3-ASR's 9, above Codex, rests on that estimate.
  • Where the timings came from. Refinement ran through the CLI Proxy API from this PC, so every cloud figure carries the same extra hop. The Local Runner rows ran on this CPU-only PC; Gemma 4 E4B is only suggested with a fast graphics card, where it is quicker than shown.
  • Refinement order. Anthropic is listed above OpenAI today. Swap them so OpenAI comes first?
