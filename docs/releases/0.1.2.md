## Changed

- The Linux AppImage now follows your desktop's widget style as well as its
  colours and icons: Kvantum themes, Adwaita on GNOME, or whatever the style
  setting in kdeglobals names. Styles the AppImage does not carry fall back
  to Breeze on Plasma and to Qt's own choice elsewhere.

## Fixed

- Dictation no longer fails with "Microphone opened but produced no audio" on
  microphones that take a moment to wake up. The shortest allowed wait is now
  500 ms; installs that had it lower are raised automatically.
- The default dictation shortcut is active right after install or upgrade on
  Plasma.
