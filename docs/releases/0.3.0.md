## Changed

- Speecher has one new interface on every platform, built with Qt Quick. It
  replaces the separate Linux, macOS and Windows apps, so all three now have
  the same pages and options. Your settings, keys, history and downloaded
  models carry over through this update.
- The app has seven pages in the order a dictation runs: Home, Transcribe
  files, Dictation, Speech to text, Refinement, Pasting and General. The
  settings pages have subpages, and search shows the path to every setting.
- The dictation popup is smaller and shows your voice as a ribbon that
  follows how loud you speak. Turn on Boring mode in Dictation › Popup &
  sounds to get the level bars back.
- Refinement defaults to Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. Claude Haiku 4.5 is
  gone.
- Speech and refinement options show Accuracy (or Quality) and Speed
  ratings, in setup and in Settings, where Speecher has measured them.

## Added

- Local models. Speech to text can run on your own computer, with no
  account: Parakeet (including Parakeet v3 for 25 European languages),
  Qwen3-ASR, Whisper Large v3 Turbo and Moonshine. They run on the CPU, on
  Vulkan on Linux and Windows, or on Metal on macOS, and on CUDA or ROCm
  when you build Speecher yourself. Refinement can use a model you run
  yourself.
- Custom Endpoints. Send speech or refinement to a server you host, such as
  Speaches 0.8.
- Fallbacks. When a speech or refinement provider is unavailable, Speecher
  tries the next one you picked. Cloud providers are skipped while you're
  offline.
- Transcribe files. Turn audio and video files into text and pick different
  models for one batch. With a local model that times its segments, such
  as Whisper, export SRT or WebVTT subtitles.
- The `speecher` command line: `listen` prints one spoken transcript,
  `transcribe` reads files or stdin (with `--srt`, `--vtt` and
  `--vocab-file`), `last` prints the last transcript, `vocabulary add`,
  `providers`, `status --json` and `status --watch`. Completions ship for
  bash, zsh and fish.
- Record calls with `speecher record start`, `status` and `stop`. The
  recording keeps your microphone as "me" and system audio as "them", with
  echo cancellation. `--mic-only` records the microphone alone.
- Dictate in languages other than English. Pick the spoken language from a
  searchable list with flags, and give each fallback its own language.
- Writing Profiles can write in another language, use their own speech
  service and refinement provider, and you can add your own.
  `speecher toggle --profile <name>` picks one for a single dictation.
- Custom refinement: extra instructions, a custom prompt, your own tones and
  cleanup levels.
- Cancel or pause a dictation, each with its own shortcut. While dictating,
  Cancel and Pause can be single keys.
- Review selection edits: see the change before a voice edit replaces the
  selected text.
- Home shows insights about your dictation: words, time saved, a heatmap and
  per-profile stats. Share them as an image, text or JSON.
- Vocabulary terms take a context, can be limited to some Writing Profiles,
  and can be marked as key terms. Snippets fill in `{date}` and `{time}`.
- A microphone test on the Microphone page.
- New refinement models: GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Haiku
  5.5, plus OpenAI's Ultrafast speed.
- Check for updates at any interval, including a custom one.
- Copy your vocabulary, profiles and refinement settings to the Android app
  by scanning a QR code. Accounts and keys are never copied.
- Rounded corners in General › Appearance, on by default.
- Linux: the sidebar blurs on KWin 6.7, and colours follow every Plasma
  colour scheme change.
- Windows: the installer adds `speecher` to your Path, ships the Visual C++
  runtime, and closes a running Speecher before installing or removing it.

## Fixed

- Dictation keeps its words when a speech stream drops, and an idle Codex
  session stays alive past its time limit.
- Speecher doesn't restart for an update while a recording runs or a
  transcript is unsaved.
- Speecher's local socket, settings and update checks are private to your
  user account, and updates accept only release versions.
- Paste and focus checks no longer stall the app while they wait.
- Linux: dictated text no longer goes into read-only fields.
- macOS: dictated text no longer goes into read-only fields.
- Linux: on Plasma, the old shortcut no longer fires while you record a new
  one.
- Linux: media paused for dictation resumes reliably afterwards.
