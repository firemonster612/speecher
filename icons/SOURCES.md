The homepage uses these marks only to show which apps Speecher types into or
which service it signs in with, and carries a "not affiliated" note. They are
unedited and tinted to one colour through a CSS mask, as Simple Icons
distributes them. The Slack mark is the exception: it is the official
full-colour file, shown as supplied.
